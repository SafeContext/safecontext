# Discount guidelines

From the deal desk. Updated September 2026.

| Discount | Who approves |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 12% | Account executive |
| 12–20% | Head of sales |
| Over 20% | CRO, with a written business case |

- Multi-year deals can go one band higher.
- Never discount the Atlas add-on in its first year.
- Don't mention unreleased features in proposals or emails. Talk about them on calls only.
