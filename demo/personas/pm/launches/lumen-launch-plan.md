# Launch plan: Lumen (smart scheduling)

**Owner:** Priya Shah · **Status:** draft · **Launch:** 14 January 2027 (not announced)

## What it is

Lumen suggests the best time to run each workflow step, based on when the people involved
are usually free. It ships to Business and Enterprise plans first.

## Timeline

| Date | Milestone |
| --- | --- |
| 18 Nov 2026 | Internal dogfood |
| 2 Dec 2026 | Beta for 50 accounts |
| 14 Jan 2027 | Public launch, blog post and webinar |

## Pricing

Included in Business and Enterprise. Starter customers can buy it for £3 per seat per month.

## Success measures

- 30% of eligible accounts turn it on within 60 days.
- Fewer than 2% of suggestions dismissed as "wrong time".
