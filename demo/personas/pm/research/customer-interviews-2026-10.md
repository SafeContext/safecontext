# Customer interviews: October 2026

Six interviews about scheduling pain, to shape Lumen.

## Northwind (logistics, 1,400 seats)

Spoke to Hannah Lewis, operations lead. Their approvals stall because approvers are in
different time zones. "Half our workflows sit overnight waiting for one person in Singapore."
Would pay for smarter routing.

## Contoso (retail, 600 seats)

Spoke to Dev Patel, head of store operations. Store managers only check Flow at the start of a
shift. They want steps to arrive then, not at 3 a.m.

## Fabrikam (manufacturing, 220 seats)

Spoke to Rosa Ortiz. Mostly happy; scheduling isn't a pain for them. Their ask is offline mode
on the factory floor, which is Helios.

## Themes

1. Time zones are the main cause of stalls (4 of 6).
2. People want steps to arrive when they're working, not when they're triggered.
3. Nobody wants another notification. Lumen must replace notifications, not add to them.
